About Us

Rooted in the Hexi Corridor

  • 38°N golden growing belt — 3,000+ sunshine hours and wide day–night temperature swings concentrate solids and flavor in onions and peppers.
  • Snowmelt irrigation from the Qilian Mountains — clean water, low pest pressure, pesticide residues controlled at the source.
  • Farm-to-plant proximity — raw material purchased and processed locally in Zhangye, Gansu, keeping freshness high and costs competitive.
  • Multi-factory network — one StoneG account covers broader categories and flexible capacity, with no single-origin risk.
Farmland at the foot of the Qilian Mountains, Zhangye — snowmelt-irrigated onion and chili fields of our partner growing bases.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Standard export packing starts from 500 kg per spec for trial orders, with mixed-loading (several specs in one container) welcome. Full container loads (20'GP / 40'GP) get the best FOB rates.

Do you provide free samples?

Yes — samples from the exact production lot are free of charge, shipped freight collect after the specification is confirmed in writing.

Which certifications do your factories hold?

Factory export licenses, HACCP / ISO 22000 and FDA (FSMA) registration documents are available on request. Certificates and COA are issued per shipment, and third-party inspection (SGS / Pony Testing) is welcome before contract.

What products do you supply?

Dehydrated white onion (kibbled 8×8 / 10×10 / 14×14 mm, powder 80–120 mesh), Mexican pepper, beetroot powder, celery, garlic, pumpkin, broccoli, okra, kale, plus dehydrated fruit powders such as apple, mango, strawberry and banana. Custom cuts and blends on request.

How fast can I get a quote?

Send your product, mesh size, volume and packing requirement — we reply with an indicative FOB range within 24 hours, and hold stock year-round on the onion lines for prompt shipment.
