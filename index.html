Why the Hexi Corridor

Our partner factories sit in Zhangye, Gansu — the middle of the Hexi Corridor, China's premier dehydrated-vegetable production region.

  • 38°N golden growing belt — 3,000+ sunshine hours and large day–night temperature swings concentrate solids and flavor in onions and peppers.
  • Snowmelt irrigation from the Qilian Mountains — clean water, low pest pressure, pesticide residues well controlled at source.
  • Farm-to-plant proximity — raw material purchased and processed locally, so freshness is high and costs stay competitive.
  • Multi-factory network — one Shigeyi account covers broader categories and flexible capacity, with no single-origin risk.

Full-process control at our partner plants

Raw-material growing bases → washing & sorting → cutting (granule / flake / powder) → hot-air drying → sieving & grading → metal detection & color sorting → export packing. One integrated line, one accountable supply chain.
