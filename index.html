Where It Grows

Why the Hexi Corridor

Our partner factories sit in Zhangye, Gansu — the middle of the Hexi Corridor, China's premier dehydrated-vegetable belt.

  • 38°N golden growing belt — 3,000+ sunshine hours and wide day–night temperature swings concentrate solids and flavor in onions and peppers.
  • Snowmelt irrigation from the Qilian Mountains — clean water, low pest pressure, pesticide residues controlled at the source.
  • Farm-to-plant proximity — raw material purchased and processed locally, so freshness is high and costs stay competitive.
  • Multi-factory network — one Shigeyi account covers broader categories and flexible capacity, with no single-origin risk.
Farmland at the foot of the Qilian Mountains, Zhangye — snowmelt-irrigated onion and chili fields of our partner growing bases.
